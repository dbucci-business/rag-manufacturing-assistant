## 1. Purpose & Scope

This document provides the master preventive maintenance (PM) schedule for all Tier 1 and Tier 2 critical assets in the Banbury Mixing, Tire Building, and Curing departments at Vulcan Tire Corporation Plant 3. PM tasks are built into eMaint as recurring work orders; this document serves as the reference standard. The Maintenance Planner is responsible for ensuring eMaint schedules match this document at all times.

PM compliance targets: 100% on-time for Tier 1 assets (Banbury mixers, tire building machines, curing presses); 95% on-time for Tier 2 assets (conveyors, stock blend lines, servicers). See VTC-MNT-KPI-001 for full KPI definitions.

## 2. Banbury Mixer PM Schedule (BB-16 Series)

### 2.1 Daily PMs – Banbury Mixers (All Units)

Performed by: Maintenance Technician (Electrical or Mechanical). Estimated time: 30–45 minutes per unit. Record completion in eMaint on PM work order generated each morning at 07:00 at the start of new production day.

| # | Task Description | Acceptance Criterion | Tools/PPE | Est. Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check hydraulic system pressure at HPU gauge | 2,800–3,050 PSI | Safety glasses | 5 min |
| 2 | Inspect dust stop seals for rubber bleed-through | No visible rubber around seal housing | Safety glasses, flashlight | 5 min |
| 3 | Verify cooling water inlet/outlet temperatures and flow rates | Per Table in VTC-EQP-BAN-003 Sec 3.3 | Safety glasses | 5 min |
| 4 | Check gearbox oil level via sight glass | Within marked operating range | Safety glasses | 3 min |
| 5 | Inspect auto-lube system reservoir level (main rotor bearings) | ≥ 50% full; lines free of kinks | Safety glasses | 5 min |
| 6 | Inspect drop door hydraulic cylinder for visible leaks | No leaks on rod or end caps | Safety glasses | 5 min |
| 7 | Verify ram position (fully retracted) prior to first batch | Ram at upper limit switch | Safety glasses | 3 min |
| 8 | Listen for abnormal noise during first mix cycle – report any bearing squeal or vibration anomaly | Normal operational sound level | Hearing protection, flashlight | 5 min |

### 2.2 Weekly PMs – Banbury Mixers

| # | Task Description | Acceptance Criterion | Tools/PPE | Est. Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grease main rotor bearing auto-lube headers (verify all lines flowing) | All lines clear, no back-pressure blockages | Safety glasses, gloves | 20 min |
| 2 | Grease ram guide bearings (10 g Mobilux EP2 each side) | Grease visible at bearing face | Grease gun, gloves | 10 min |
| 3 | Apply Molykote 111 to dust stop seal lip areas | Thin even film, no excess | Brush, gloves, safety glasses | 15 min |
| 4 | Check hydraulic oil level in HPU reservoir | Within operating band on sight glass | Safety glasses | 5 min |
| 5 | Verify hydraulic filter delta-P indicator (not in red zone) | Indicator in green/yellow zone | Safety glasses | 3 min |
| 6 | Inspect all hydraulic hoses for cracks, abrasion, or bulging | No cracks, abrasion through cover, or bulging | Safety glasses, flashlight | 10 min |
| 7 | Torque check on drop door clamping bolts (sample 4 bolts) | 180 Nm per engineering spec | Torque wrench, safety glasses | 15 min |

## 3. Tire Building Machine (TBM) PM Schedule

Asset Class: McNeil TBM-17 Series single-stage tire building machines. Asset IDs: VTC-P3-GTB-001 through VTC-P3-GTB-012. Tier 1 Critical assets.

### 3.1 Daily PMs – TBMs

| # | Task Description | Acceptance Criterion | Tools/PPE | Est. Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect stitcher roll assembly for wear and alignment | No flat spots; aligned per setup specs | Safety glasses | 8 min |
| 2 | Check all air bladder inflation pressures (building drum and transfer ring) | Per machine setup card ± 0.1 bar | Safety glasses, pressure gauge | 10 min |
| 3 | Verify drum expansion/contraction cycle (manual jog test) | Full stroke in < 4 seconds | Safety glasses | 5 min |
| 4 | Clean all servicer tracks and apply light oil where specified | Tracks clear of rubber debris | Safety glasses, gloves, oil can | 10 min |

## 4. Curing Press PM Schedule

Asset Class: McNeil-Akron P-48 Series hydraulic curing presses. Asset IDs: VTC-P3-CUR-001 through VTC-P3-CUR-024. Tier 1 Critical assets. Curing presses operate at temperatures up to 185°C (365°F) and pressures up to 250 PSI steam. All PM tasks require proper burn/steam PPE.

### 4.1 Daily PMs – Curing Presses

| # | Task Description | Acceptance Criterion | Tools/PPE | Est. Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect mold faces for buildup of compound flash or mold release agent residue | Clean mold face per VTC-OPS-MOLD-009 | Heat-resist gloves, face shield | 10 min |
| 2 | Check steam supply and condensate return trap operation | Trap cycling normally (test valve); no condensate backup | Safety glasses, gloves | 5 min |
| 3 | Verify bladder inflation nitrogen pressure gauge reading | Set pressure per cure card ± 5 PSI | Safety glasses | 5 min |
| 4 | Inspect lower platen guide pins and bushings for wear | < 0.25 mm radial play | Safety glasses, dial indicator | 8 min |
| 5 | Confirm all safety light curtains and interlocks are active and untampered | All safety indicators green on HMI | Safety glasses | 3 min |
