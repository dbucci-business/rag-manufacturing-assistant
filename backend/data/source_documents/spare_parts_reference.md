## 1. Purpose & Scope

This document is the master spare parts reference for critical spare components maintained in the Vulcan Tire Corporation Plant 3 maintenance storeroom. It covers Tier 1 asset critical spares for Banbury mixers, tire building machines (TBMs), and curing presses. All parts listed are maintained at minimum stock levels to support planned PM work and emergency breakdowns. Part numbers, preferred suppliers, and reorder points are maintained in eMaint Inventory and in SAP MM module.

Storeroom operating hours: Monday–Friday 06:00–22:00; Saturday 06:00–14:00. After-hours parts access requires on-call supervisor authorization. All storeroom issues must be recorded against a work order in eMaint within the same shift.

## 5. Storeroom Management Rules

### 5.1 Inventory Accuracy

eMaint inventory must reflect physical storeroom stock at all times. Monthly cycle counts are conducted on Tier 1 asset critical spares. Annual physical inventory of the full storeroom occurs in December. Inventory discrepancies greater than 2 units or $500 must be investigated and resolved within 5 business days.

### 5.2 Critical Spare Identification

All critical spares on this list are flagged in eMaint with the "Critical Spare" attribute. These parts cannot be used for non-critical applications without Maintenance Manager approval. Critical spare status is reviewed annually during the reliability review and updated based on failure history and lead time changes.

### 5.3 Shelf Life & Storage Requirements

- Elastomeric seals, bladders, and o-rings: Maximum 2-year shelf life; store in temperature-controlled (15–25°C) location away from UV light and ozone sources (motors, welders)
- Bearings: Store in original packaging until use; inspect for corrosion before installation
- Hydraulic filters: Store in sealed packaging; protect from dust and moisture
- Lubricants: Rotate on FIFO basis; check expiry dates quarterly
